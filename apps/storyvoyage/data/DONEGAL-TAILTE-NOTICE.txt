These are approximate institutional planning references for the existing
Donegal Irish catalogue identity, not all County Donegal or Ireland and not
exact, exhaustive, uniform or exclusive dialect boundaries. They are not a
printing-specific territory claim, speaker census, acquisition place, GPS
confirmation or personal visit. Speakers may live outside planning areas;
not every location within them has speakers. Publication and edit dates are
administrative metadata, not a 2026 linguistic survey. The reference-quality
weight is not a population percentage.
